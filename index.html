About

Built for the inflection point between scrappy and scalable.

Stage Two Partners exists for companies at the critical moment when early-stage momentum needs to become a repeatable, scalable product organization — without losing the speed and ownership that got you here.

Led by Aaron Cripps, we bring senior PM expertise earned across the full spectrum: from founding teams and seed-stage companies growing through their first real product challenge, all the way through Series A and beyond — including years building product at Apple.

We don't arrive with a slide deck of frameworks. We work alongside your team, understand your specific constraints and culture, and help you build the capabilities that fit where you're actually going — not where a generic playbook says you should be.
